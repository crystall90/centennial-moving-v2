FAQ

Questions before you book

Where do you move?

Long-distance and interprovincial moves across Canada, coast to coast. Our warehouses and trucks are based in Toronto, Calgary and Vancouver.

How is my move protected?

Basic released-value protection at $0.60 per lb is included. Extended protection is available with a $300.00 deductible. Claims are accepted within 7 days of delivery at claims@centennialmoving.ca.

Can you store my things between homes?

Yes. We offer short- and long-term storage in heated warehouses in Toronto, Calgary and Vancouver.

Can you ship my car too?

Yes. Vehicle transport can be added to your household move or booked on its own.

How do I get an exact price?

Request a free quote with your route, move size and date. Your coordinator confirms the inventory with you and gives you an estimate based on it.
